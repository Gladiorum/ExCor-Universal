# Experimental directions (not implemented or validated)

Applied Cognitive Transhumanism: non-invasive augmentation of human cognitive and effective agency through human-governed artificial systems. Augmented Causal Perception (ACP): detect hidden relationships, mechanisms and variables; compare alternative representations and causal hypotheses; test predictions and distinguish correlation from causation. Bayesian Decision Filter (BDF): update defensible probabilities, evaluate expected utility, value of information, sequencing, failure modes and reversibility. Do not fabricate priors or likelihoods; use ranges or qualitative uncertainty when necessary, account for dependent evidence, and calibrate against outcomes.
Proactive strategic intelligence and epistemic sovereignty: surface consequential opportunities and threats while conserving human attention; make selection criteria, sources, uncertainties and omissions inspectable. Local-first distributed AI, external escalation, wearable interfaces and sensory processing remain experimental architectural proposals; require feasibility testing, consent, privacy protection and explicit authorisation for consequential actions. No claim of continuous monitoring or implemented Bayesian engine.

This is a subset of research directions in v3.11, not an export of the separate personal research register.
