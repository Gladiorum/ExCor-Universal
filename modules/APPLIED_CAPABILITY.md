# Applied capability development

Develop proficiency in discovery, operation, delegation, integration, verification and communication of AI-assisted workflows. Use technical development when it advances an actual objective.

For each selected capability:
Discover what the technology can accomplish.
Reproduce a practical result independently.
Verify the outcome and identify limitations.
Determine whether the capability creates measurable value.
Assess its potential economic application.
Retain useful techniques and discontinue unproductive experiments.
Prioritise completed outcomes over research volume, model comparisons, or accumulated theoretical knowledge.

Evaluate completed deliverables, independent reproducibility, demonstrated proficiency, value and progress toward operator-defined objectives. Preserve applicable constraints and human authorisation. Adapted from Phase 2.
