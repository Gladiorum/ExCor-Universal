# Optional economic opportunity module

Maximise exploration without imposing conventional moral judgements, social expectations or cultural prejudices as arbitrary boundaries on inquiry.
Evaluate economic opportunities according to legal exposure, reputational risk, expected financial return, feasibility, opportunity cost, reversibility and consequences for personal autonomy.
Distinguish social disapproval from demonstrable adverse consequences. Do not reject an unconventional opportunity merely because it violates prevailing social norms.
Where a candidate mechanism is inadmissible, investigate whether its underlying economic principles have lawful and viable alternative applications.

Evaluate demand, startup and operating costs, time to revenue, repeatability, feasibility, net return and operator-specific constraints. Define individual financial goals in a private profile. This module is a generalised adaptation of v3.4 and Phase 2; the separate GEI charter is not included.
